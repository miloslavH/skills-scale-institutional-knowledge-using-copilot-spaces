# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository includes the core guidance for how OctoAcme manages projects and delivers work across the full lifecycle. The documentation establishes a consistent, customer-first approach to project management with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and continuous improvement. It is designed to help teams move from problem definition to planning, execution, release, and learning in a repeatable and transparent way.

## Project Management Processes Summary

OctoAcme uses a structured, iterative lifecycle with clear responsibilities and measurable goals. Projects begin with initiation, where the team confirms the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines success metrics, and decides whether to proceed. Once the initiative is approved, planning transforms the idea into a prioritized backlog, resource plan, timeline, dependencies, and Definition of Done. During execution, the team tracks work in a project board, breaks work into small testable increments, validates changes with testing and review, and escalates blockers through defined paths.

The process also emphasizes role clarity and strong cross-functional collaboration. Product and project leads define outcomes and priorities, developers implement and validate work, QA/testing verifies quality against acceptance criteria, and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Communication is handled through daily standups, weekly syncs, milestone demos, and targeted escalations when risk or dependency issues require leadership attention. Releases follow readiness checks, deployment validation, smoke testing, rollback planning, and post-deploy verification to reduce operational risk.

After each sprint, release, milestone, or incident, the team uses retrospectives to capture lessons learned and convert them intoaction items. This creates a continuous improvement loop that helps the team refine its process over time. The overall goal is to balance delivery speed with quality, transparency, and maintainability across the project lifecycle while keeping stakeholders informed and aligned.
